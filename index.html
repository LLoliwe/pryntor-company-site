What Pryntor does

Pryntor is an independent technology venture based in Durban, South Africa. We explore software, learning tools, original creative experiences, AI evaluation and robotics simulation. These projects include early research and prototypes, not a catalogue of products cleared for commercial release. We aim to explain limitations honestly, test failures, and invite independent scrutiny before describing work as ready. This site offers information and contact by email, not customer accounts, automated purchasing or a public integration API.

What stays in the page

The guide matches questions to fixed, local answers. It has no external model connection. Messages are held in page memory only, are not saved to cookies or browser storage, and can be removed using Clear chat or by reloading. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

Voice is optional

No microphone starts automatically. Voice input is restricted to a browser that reports already-installed on-device recognition. Unsupported browsers stay text-only. Read aloud uses only a voice marked as local by your browser. There is no cloud fallback or automatic language download. Browser and operating-system behaviour still requires independent device verification.

Contacting Pryntor

The email link opens your email application. Pryntor does not send an email for you. Sending from that application uses your email provider and the recipient's email system, which have separate data handling. No delivery or response time is promised here.
