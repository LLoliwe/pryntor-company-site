03 / How we work

Make something you can try.

The approach
Start with a clear problem. Build a working prototype that makes the idea concrete.
The work
An original build, with a clear purpose and a version we can return to.
The standard
Be clear about what works today and what is still in development.

Look for what breaks.

The approach
Test the awkward cases as well as the obvious ones. Consider accessibility, privacy and misuse from the start.
The work
A record of what we tested, what happened and what needs attention.
The standard
Treat a passing test as evidence, not a guarantee.

Make the result checkable.

The approach
Explain what a result shows, how we reached it and where its limits are.
The work
Evidence that lets someone else examine the claim.
The standard
Let the evidence set the scale of the claim.

Learn from what did not work.

The approach
Keep the failures as well as the successes. Use both to decide what changes next.
The work
A dated record of the result, including the parts that fell short.
The standard
Make the next version better informed than the last.
