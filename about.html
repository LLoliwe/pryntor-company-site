Useful technology, tested with care.

Pryntor is an independent technology venture based in Durban, South Africa. We explore software, learning tools, original creative experiences, AI evaluation and robotics simulation. These projects include early research and prototypes, not a catalogue of products cleared for commercial release. We aim to explain limitations honestly, test failures, and invite independent scrutiny before describing work as ready. This site offers information and contact by email, not customer accounts, automated purchasing or a public integration API.

What we work on

Our areas of exploration include original software and web experiences, educational products and teaching tools, AI evaluation and reasoning research, visual storytelling, and robotics simulation. We distinguish engineering demonstrations from validated hardware, learner outcomes, commercial services and public products. The current website is an introduction to this work rather than an order form or a promise of a release date.

How we work

We look for a clear user problem, create an original prototype, inspect the mechanisms behind successful reference work, and test where our ideas fail. Independent expertise, accessibility, privacy and proper source attribution matter. Tests or visually compelling demonstrations alone do not establish that a product is safe, released or independently approved.

Contact and trust

For a general question, research conversation or possible collaboration, contact hello@pryntor.co.za. The main site does not accept accounts, payments, direct bookings, uploaded private records or developer API requests. Its optional browser assistant uses local scripted responses rather than a hosted generative AI model.
