# Security policy (template: needs Lebo's approval before publishing; Tirisano also needs Mpho Loliwe's approval)

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email security@<domain> (or lebo@pryntor.co.za until that exists). Please include steps to reproduce and the URL affected. We aim to acknowledge within 5 working days.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, stay within this policy, avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service disruption, and give us reasonable time to fix before disclosure, we will not pursue action against you for your research.

## Out of scope

Denial of service, social engineering, physical attacks, third-party services we use (report to them), and findings from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.

We do not currently offer paid bounties.
