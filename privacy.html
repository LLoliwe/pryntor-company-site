Contact

What we collect and why

We only process the information you choose to send us by email, such as your name, email address and message. We use it to reply to you, to keep a record of the conversation and, if you ask, to tell you when we launch. We do not sell personal information or use it for automated decisions.

Early list

If you join the early list on our homepage and tick the consent box, we store your email address, the date you joined, a referral code and who referred you, so we can show your place in line and email you about our launch. Until our list service is switched on, joining works by sending us an email instead. The list is held with our database provider, Supabase, under its data processing terms, and may be stored outside South Africa with appropriate safeguards. We keep it until launch communications end or you unsubscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every email or by writing to hello@pryntor.co.za.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold your personal information, request access to it, ask us to correct or delete it, object to its processing, and withdraw consent you gave earlier. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time after verifying your identity.

Complaints

If you believe your personal information has been mishandled, please tell us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za, Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191.
