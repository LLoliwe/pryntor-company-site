Privacy notice

Updated 18 October 2026 · Pryntor (Pty) Ltd, Durban, South Africa

This is the notice for Pryntor's informational corporate website candidate. Publication and independent legal, hosting, and privacy review are still pending. This page does not certify regulatory compliance.

1. Who is responsible

Pryntor (Pty) Ltd is responsible for information provided directly to us through the contact channels shown on this website. For privacy questions and data requests, email info@pryntor.co.za.

2. Information we receive

This website candidate currently has no user registration, checkout or contact form. If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and anything you voluntarily include. Please avoid sending passwords, children's information, financial details or other sensitive personal information by ordinary email.

Our website hosting and email providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, browser details, access logs and message metadata to operate, secure and troubleshoot their services. Their configurations and retention periods must be checked before publication.

3. Why we use it

We use enquiries to respond to you, handle legitimate business correspondence, protect our systems and comply with applicable legal obligations. We will not treat someone who merely emailed us as a sales lead without an appropriate lawful basis and a suitable consent or objection process.

4. Cookies and analytics

The reviewed static source version does not contain third-party analytics, advertising trackers or a cookie-banner-dependent feature. The live hosting environment and any future embedded media, measurement tools or marketing technology must be separately checked before this statement is used publicly.

5. Sharing and international services

Service providers supporting email, hosting or security may process data to perform those functions. Any international transfers must be reviewed against applicable data-protection requirements. We do not publish a list of providers until their actual contracts and configurations have been verified.

6. Security, retention and your choices

We aim to restrict access to business correspondence and retain it only as long as needed for lawful purposes, legitimate business operations or recordkeeping. If you want to ask whether we hold your personal information, request correction or deletion, or raise a concern, email the contact address above. Requests may be subject to legally permitted exceptions and identity verification.

7. Children

This corporate information website is intended for business and adult enquiries. It does not offer a child account or child-directed interactive service. ZOWIBO and other child-facing products require their own specialist privacy and safeguarding assessments before any launch.

8. Updates

We may revise this notice when the live website, service providers or features change. The effective version should show the date of its latest review.
