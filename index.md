# Pryntor

Pryntor is an independent technology venture based in Durban, South Africa.

Pryntor is an independent technology venture based in Durban, South Africa. We explore software, learning tools, original creative experiences, AI evaluation and robotics simulation. These projects include early research and prototypes, not a catalogue of products cleared for commercial release. We aim to explain limitations honestly, test failures, and invite independent scrutiny before describing work as ready. This site offers information and contact by email, not customer accounts, automated purchasing or a public integration API.

## Focus areas

- Software and web experiences, including early-stage product exploration.
- Education and learning tools. No accreditation or measured student outcomes are claimed here.
- AI evaluation, reasoning and other research. Prototypes are not independently approved production systems.
- Creative technology and original visual storytelling.
- Robotics simulation and emerging systems. Simulated behaviour is not evidence of deployed or safety-certified hardware.

## How we work

We call our method the proof chain: change, test, prove, and keep the failures. We begin with a clear user problem, create an original prototype, examine how strong examples solve that problem, test failures, and seek independent review before a release claim. Source attribution, privacy, accessibility and meaningful human oversight are core review concerns.

## Ventures

Pryntor builds its own ventures across these areas. A venture is named publicly only after it clears four gates: rights cleared, privacy and safety reviewed, accessible, and independently checked. None has been released publicly yet.
